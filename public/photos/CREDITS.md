# Photo sources

These files are served locally; the visitor makes no requests to image services.

- `together.webp`: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1516589178581-6cd7833ae3b2
- `sunset.webp`: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1474552226712-ac0f0961a954
- `stars.webp`: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1519681393784-d120267933ba

The three scene photographs are used under the Unsplash license: https://unsplash.com/license (accessed 2026-11-15). Downloading, modifying and using images for commercial and noncommercial purposes is permitted. Images are not offered for resale.

`personal-*.webp` are optimized, EXIF-stripped copies of the five photographs supplied in this project's `photos/` folder. These personal photos are not represented as Unsplash assets. Originals remain untouched and are excluded from Git. Replace or remove them via `src/config/loveConfig.ts`.

The heart artwork, social preview layout, and synthesized audio are original to this project. Fonts are locally hosted Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans, distributed under the SIL Open Font License through their Fontsource packages.
